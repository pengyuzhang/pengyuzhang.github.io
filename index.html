Pengyu Zhang


I am a Senior Engineering Manager at Uber, leading teams that build data pipelines for large-scale data lakes and develop machine learning models for threat detection and AI safety. My research interests include large-scale distributed systems, machine learning, LLMs, and security/safety.

Previously, I led cloud infrastructure and platform development at Alibaba Cloud Seattle. I received my Ph.D. from UMass Amherst (advised by Deepak Ganesan) and was a postdoctoral researcher at Stanford (with Sachin Katti), earning the ACM SIGMOBILE Doctoral Dissertation Award for work on wireless systems. I hold bachelor's and master's degrees from Tsinghua University.
